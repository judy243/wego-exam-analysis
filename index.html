Finding · 01

Mathematics drops sharpest from grade 7 to grade 10, but the top-quartile climbs to 91.4 in G10 自然組 — the strongest single-subject mean in the dataset.

Finding · 02

Grade 7 地理 posts a 96.76 高標 and the smallest standard deviation (10.58) — the most consistently high-performing sitting.

Finding · 03

Grade 8 理化 and Grade 9 理化 sit as the structural weak points — both below 70 mean, with almost a third of G8 below the 70 band.
